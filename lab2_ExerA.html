About the Event

Programming Workshop 2026 is a one-day event organised to help students improve their programming skills, explore new technologies and learn from experienced speakers.

Speaker 1: Dr. Nurul Nazihah Jamal

Dr. Nurul Nazihah Jamal is a software engineer who specialises in web development. He will introduce students to the fundamentals of HTML and website development.

Speaker 2: Ms. Farhana Nafisa Binti Azhar

Ms. Farhana Nafisa Binti Azhar is an application developer who will share practical programming techniques and guide students through a hands-on coding activity.
