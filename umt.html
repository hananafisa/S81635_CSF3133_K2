Welcome to UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. Originally established as a centre for fisheries and marine science studies under Universiti Pertanian Malaysia, it has grown into a premier focal point for marine science, aquatic resources, and oceanography in the region.

Committed to academic excellence and sustainable development, UMT offers a diverse range of undergraduate and postgraduate programmes, fostering innovation, research excellence, and community engagement to shape future-ready graduates.
